About Digi2U · 501(c)(3) Nonprofit

Bridging the
digital divide.

The tools that turn a skill into a living sit behind a paywall, a campus gate, or a job you cannot get without already having the skill. Digi2U puts them in the neighborhood and opens the door — hands-on training in technology, media, and the creative industries, built for working adults.

Mission & Vision

Empowering communities through innovation.

Everyone who walks in here arrives with something already — a trade, a hustle, years of work, a good eye. Digi2U, a 501(c)(3), builds on that instead of asking anyone to start from zero. Training in STEAM: Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Math.

M

Our Mission

To equip our communities with the tools, training, and opportunities to succeed.

V

Our Vision

A world where all individuals, regardless of background, have access to the resources needed to thrive in a technology-driven society.
