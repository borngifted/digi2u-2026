01 The Opportunity

Bridging the
digital divide.

Ninety-two percent of jobs now ask for digital skills. A third of the workforce has never been handed the chance to build them. That is not a talent gap — it is a door, and in our communities it has been closed a long time.

These are national workforce figures from the Bureau of Labor Statistics and the National Skills Coalition. They describe the landscape Digi2U operates in — they are not Digi2U's results.

04 Hands On Always

Real tools. Real projects.
Real outcomes.

Theory matters. But nobody ever learned to run a four-camera shoot from a slide deck. Every track ends in something that exists — a printed prototype, a mastered track, a finished film, a licensed flight.

Why This Matters

The neighborhood is being rewritten. Ours is learning to write.

Digi2U trains adults in Newark and Jersey City — the same blocks that 26 Blocks is spending twenty years documenting.

That documentary series is about gentrification: who gets pushed out, who gets to stay, and who gets to decide. Digi2U provides the technical expertise, the infrastructure, and the training in media production and documentary filmmaking that makes the work possible — through makerspaces in Jersey City, Newark, and Atlanta.

So the people whose neighborhoods are being rewritten are the ones learning to hold the camera. That is not a nice coincidence. It is the whole argument: skills are how a community stops being the subject of somebody else's story and starts telling its own.
