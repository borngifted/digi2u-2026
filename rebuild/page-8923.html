About Digi2U: Empowering Communities Through Innovation

Digi2U is dedicated to providing hands-on training in technology, media, and creative industries to empower individuals and communities. Through our innovative programs, we equip students with the skills, tools, and resources needed to succeed in the digital age, fostering growth, creativity, and career advancement.

 

Digi2U, a nonprofit 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, prepares people for well-paid technology jobs and in-demand businesses through innovative training in STEAM (science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Math) learning and application. 

OUR MISSION

To equip our communities with the tools, training, and opportunities to succeed

OUR VISION: A world where all individuals, regardless of backgroung, have access to the resources needed to thrive in a technology driven society.
Welcome

Digi2U is working to empower communities by providing them with access to cutting-edge technology, training, and opportunities, enabling individuals to excel in the digital age. We seek to foster sustainable communities where success is within reach for everyone, regardless of their background.

OUR MASTER PLAN

Our master plan is to develop, provide, and host a network of resources through our website at www.digi2u, our community campus network and collaborations with other organizations and people in our communities that include northeast New Jersey, southwest Atlanta. Our innovative learning opportunities with business and media production enrichment services empower…, to facilitate collaborative workflows through shared access to the tools of business and content production and encourage giving back through mentorship, volunteerism, apprenticeship, and sponsorship. By sharing technology and work resources collaboratively our members, collectively and individually, are better positioned to compete in the modern economy and uplift economies and individuals in need.

OUR STORY

Empowering Communities Through Innovation

Digi2U model is easily  expandable, replicatible yet also easily tailored to local and specific needs. We have successful reached 1000’s through our community initiatives and have created curriculum, invested in tools and space, and trained over 25 adults and youth in year long intensive’s resulting in graduates with many business and career advancement and youth now in university.  Our collaboration model we operate in five cities and work and meet through our advanced virtual environment our community. 

Our programs are designed for our communities, and have been tested through our successful initiatives serving 1000's through community initiatives and dozens in media, additive manufacturing and wood working training intensives. With collaborative spirit, and your support, our approach to programming can become self-sustainable, and can be replicated, expanded and tailored to our communities' objectives.
