A Documentary Exploring Gentrification & Community Impact

26 Blocks Doc: Uncovering Stories of Resilience & Change

26 Blocks Doc is a powerful documentary series that delves into the realities of gentrification and its effects on African American communities. Through in-depth storytelling, interviews, and historical context, we highlight the voices of community leaders, developers, and residents fighting to reclaim their neighborhoods amidst ongoing change.

How Digi2U Supports 26Blocks.com

We are In a Mission to Help the Helpless

Digi2U plays a pivotal role in supporting 26Blocks.com by providing the technical expertise, infrastructure, and collaborative framework needed to bring its vision to life. With a mission rooted in empowering underrepresented communities through STEAM education, Digi2U brings a wealth of resources and innovative approaches that align seamlessly with the goals of 26 Blocks.

A Partnership Rooted in Empowerment

26Blocks.com tells the stories of diverse communities, shedding light on their creativity, resilience, and culture. Digi2U amplifies this mission by offering cutting-edge tools and training in media production and documentary filmmaking. Through our makerspaces in Jersey City, Newark, and Atlanta, we provide the infrastructure needed for creators to capture, edit, and share powerful narratives. Our collaboration with 26Blocks.com ensures access to top-tier digital resources, fostering the development of meaningful, high-quality storytelling that reflects the essence of the communities it highlights.

ELVIN DOMINIC

Elvin Dominici is a community advocate and leader who ran for an at-large seat on the Jersey City Council in the 2021 general election, demonstrating his commitment to public service and bettering his community. Although he did not win, his dedication to creating positive change remains steadfast. Currently, 26Blocks is working on a biopic that highlights Elvin’s inspiring journey, capturing his passion for service, resilience, and vision for the future.
Produced by:26 Blocks
