Digi2U  is 501(c)(3) tax-exempt non- profit  organization that prepares individuals with hands on training  in STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Math) fields. We focus on  the needs of those in the community looking for opportunity to develop themselves with a specialized skillset to start their own business or become gain fully employed. 

Teaching Tomorrow's Technology Today

  • Access to Technology: Cutting-edge tools for all.
  • Skill Building: Hands on training opportunities.
  • Empowerment: Skill set building and job creation.
  • Sustainability: Building Maker-spaces in local Communities.
About us

Education Focused to Serve The Need

We design and implement immersive, hands-on eduction that includes real-life projects, situation awareness training and team building through joint collaborations  and initiatives that benefit the community.

We are developing , Mobile Classrooms , Virtual Enviroments and Makerspaces for learning that equip individuals with  cutting edge tool access that meets today competitive work requirements.

Our Programs
Build and Master Your Own 3D Printer
3D Design & Fabrication

Learn the fundamentals of 3D modeling, printing, and fabrication techniques to bring creative ideas to life. This program covers design software, prototyping, and production processes used in industries like manufacturing, engineering, and product design.

Mix and Master Professional-Quality Sound
Audio Production

Master the art of sound recording, editing, and mixing for music, podcasts, and media production. Learn to work with industry-standard software and professional audio equipment to produce high-quality sound.

 

Unleash Creativity Through Digital & Visual Arts
Art & Design

Develop skills in graphic design, digital illustration, and branding using Adobe Creative Suite, Corel Draw, and other industry tools. Perfect for artists looking to expand their creative expertise in digital and print media.

Master Drone Flight & Aerial Photography
Drone Operation & Aerial Imaging

Gain hands-on experience in drone operation, aerial photography, and mapping technology. This program covers FAA regulations, drone flight techniques, and commercial applications in filmmaking, real estate, and surveying.

Design, Create, and Customize Apparel
Fashion & Design

Explore the creative and technical aspects of fashion production, including embroidery, screen printing, vinyl cutting, and sublimation. Learn to design, source materials, and manufacture custom apparel. reclaiming community, only to have their efforts thwarted for someone else’s agenda.

Tell Stories Through Film, TV & Digital Media
Film & Television Production

Train in cinematography, directing, screenwriting, and video editing to create compelling film and TV projects. Work with professional cameras, lighting setups, and post-production software to develop storytelling techniques for digital media.

Creative Threads
Grants Program

Receive financial support and training opportunities through grant-funded programs in media production, technology, and business development. Learn how to apply for grants and leverage funding for education and career advancement.

Balance Through Fitness, Nutrition & Mindfulness
Health & Wellness

Focus on physical, mental, and emotional well-being through fitness training, nutrition, stress management, and mindfulness. This program promotes sustainable health practices to improve overall lifestyle and community well-being.

Grow Your Brand with Digital & Social Strategies
Marketing & Brand Strategy

Learn how to develop, promote, and grow a brand using social media marketing, content creation, SEO, and advertising strategies. This course is ideal for entrepreneurs, business owners, and marketing professionals.

 

Stunning Images with Professional Techniques
Photography

Gain skills in camera operation, lighting, composition, and photo editing to create high-quality images. Learn portrait, commercial, event, and storytelling photography techniques while building a professional portfolio.

Broadcast Your Voice & Engage Audiences
Podcast & Streaming Production

Master audio recording, live broadcasting, and digital storytelling for podcasts and live-streamed content. Learn how to use professional microphones, recording software, and audience engagement strategies to grow an online presence.

Build, Design, and Execute Stage Setups
Staging & Rigging for Live Events

Learn stage design, rigging safety, lighting installation, and event setup for concerts, theater productions, and live broadcasts. Gain technical expertise in stage construction, equipment handling, and event production management.
