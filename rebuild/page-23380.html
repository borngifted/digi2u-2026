ServSafe Certification: Master Food Safety & Sanitation

The ServSafe Certification Training is a comprehensive food safety program developed by the National Restaurant Association. It is designed to educate food service workers and managers on proper food handling, hygiene, and sanitation practices to ensure public health and compliance with food safety regulations.

Course Overview:

The ServSafe Food Safety Certification Training is a nationally recognized food safety program designed to provide essential knowledge in food handling, hygiene, and sanitation. Participants will learn proper food storage, allergen safety, cross-contamination prevention, and regulatory compliance. With two certification levels—Food Handler and Food Protection Manager—this course is perfect for restaurant staff, catering professionals, and food service managers looking to enhance food safety standards.

  • Course Level: Beginner
  • Skills: Food Handler Certification

Class Projects

Project: Food Safety Inspection & Sanitation Plan Students will complete a food safety evaluation, analyzing a kitchen or food service area for sanitation, hygiene, and cross-contamination risks. They will then develop a comprehensive sanitation plan, detailing proper cleaning schedules, temperature control methods, and best practices for maintaining a safe and compliant food service environment.
