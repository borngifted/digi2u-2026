Camera Operation & Manual Settings

Learn how to control exposure, focus, and shutter speed to capture high-quality images in any environment.

Lighting Techniques

Composition & Framing

Portfolio Development

Course description:

Our Photography Course provides a comprehensive education in professional photography, including:

  • Camera operation and manual settings for optimal image capture.
  • Lighting techniques for natural and studio photography.
  • Composition and framing to create visually striking images.
  • Post-production editing using industry-standard software like Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop.
  • Portfolio development to showcase your work professionally.

Students will work on real-world projects, refining their photography skills through studio sessions, outdoor shoots, and client-based assignments.
