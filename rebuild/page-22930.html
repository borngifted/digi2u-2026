Master the Art of Fashion & Design

Our Fashion & Design program is an immersive training experience that equips students with the skills to design, create, and produce apparel using industry-standard tools and techniques. Whether you’re an aspiring fashion designer, apparel entrepreneur, or creative artist, this program provides the knowledge and resources to bring your visions to life.

What You'll Learn

In our Fashion & Design program, you'll gain hands-on experience in apparel creation, from digital design to garment production. Learn embroidery, screen printing, vinyl cutting, and sublimation while mastering industry-standard software. Develop the technical skills and creative expertise to bring your fashion ideas to life.

Fundamentals of Apparel Design

Gain a deep understanding of design principles, industry terminology, and creative workflows.

Introduction to Design Software

Learn to create digital artwork using industry-standard tools such as:

  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Photoshop
  • Corel Draw
  • Canva
  • Stitch Era Embroidery Software

Vinyl Cutting & Application

Master safety procedures, design workflows, and garment applications using assorted vinyl finishes (matte, gloss, flock, glow-in-the-dark, puff, etc.).

Embroidery Techniques

Learn to digitize designs, thread machines, and execute precise embroidery on various materials, including hats, jackets, shirts, and sneakers.

Screen Printing

Develop skills in ink application, screen preparation, and garment printing techniques for professional-quality apparel.

Sublimation Printing

Explore the process of transferring high-quality designs onto fabrics, mugs, glassware, and other materials.

Course Description

Our Fashion & Design Course is designed to immerse students in the apparel design industry, providing technical skills and creative direction. This 6-12 week training program includes:

  • Hands-on experience with industry-standard machines such as vinyl cutters, embroidery machines, sublimation printers, and heat presses.
  • Creative workflow training, covering design principles, material sourcing, and production techniques.
  • Practical application of fashion technology in custom garment creation, branding, and merchandising.

Students will work on real-world projects, refining their skills and building a portfolio that showcases their unique style and expertise.
