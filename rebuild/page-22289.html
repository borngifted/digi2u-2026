Prototyping and 3D Design

Gain foundational skills in 3D design and printing with this beginner-level course. Learn to design 3D models, build and operate 3D printers, and master essential techniques to create high-quality projects. This program is ideal for those looking to start a career in engineering or enhance their technical skills.

Level 1 Basic Training

Course Overview:

This course will provide students with a basic understanding of 3D design and printing. They will be learning how to design 3D model in cad software. Students will have the opportunity to build a 3D printer and develop a basic understanding of the machine, machine operation, design elements and understand best practices. The course will teach students the foundational knowledge needed to start producing quality 3D models to sell and gain employment in engineering fields.

  • Course Level: Beginner
  • Skills: 3D Design and Engineering

Class Projects

Learn to design and create innovative product prototypes like bottles and packaging, craft functional and stylish furniture through structural design, explore the art of lifelike human body sculpting with humanoid modeling, and develop precision-engineered automotive parts that emphasize performance and practicality.

Product

Design and create innovative product prototypes such as bottles and packaging, combining functionality with aesthetic appeal.

Structural

Craft functional and visually appealing furniture designs, focusing on structure, stability, and creativity.

Humanoid

Explore the art of human body sculpting by designing detailed and lifelike humanoid models.

Automotive

Design functional automotive parts with precision, emphasizing performance and practicality.

Course description:

In the course the student will learn overall basic knowledge pertaining to terminology, software, and best practices.

  • Terminology
  • Software (CAD & Slicer)
  • Safety and best Practice
  • Machine Operation

Course description:

In the course the student will build their own 3d printer from start to finish.

  • Frame Assembly
  • Electrical Components
  • Motors X Y Z
  • Heated Bed
  • Arduino (Brain)
  • Control Screen

Course description:

In the course the student will learn how to design objects in Tinkercad (3d Design Software)

  • Text
  • Shapes
  • Layering
  • Object depth

Course description:

In this course students will learn how to setup files for 3d printing.

  • Slicing
  • Machine Calibration
  • Heated bed vs Cold Bed printing
  • File formats
  • svg, obj, and stl exporting
