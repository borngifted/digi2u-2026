Master Staging & Rigging for Live Events

Our Staging & Rigging program provides hands-on training in stage setup, rigging safety, lighting installation, and structural design for live events, concerts, theater productions, and broadcasts. This program equips students with the technical skills and safety knowledge required to work behind the scenes in event production, stage management, and rigging operations.

With access to professional-grade equipment, real-world projects, and expert instructors, participants will learn how to construct stable stage environments, rig lighting and audio setups, and ensure safety compliance for high-profile productions.
