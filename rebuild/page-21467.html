Digi2U Events: Experience Innovation in Action

Digi2u focuses on giving our student the best exposure to the various industries via field trips, industry events, seminars, expos and workshop to help advance their knowledge and gain exposure to  industry professionals and major company’s pioneering  the way of the future of technology. This trips give our students the ability to open their eyes to the possibilities of future partnerships, employment, and sponsorships opportunities.

The goal is to give our participants all possible ways to engage their passion, through hands on learning, exploration, and application. With your support you will aid  the future leaders with the ability to gain exposure and access to life changing experiences.

Thank you to all who have supported and volunteered their time energy and effort in making this possible. Please donate to this cause and help change and impact the life of individuals that need just space and opportunity to grow.

Below you can see coming soon events and experience our members will be attending due to you generous support.

Join  also our  hands-on workshops, ndustry panels, and community-driven events designed to inspire, educate, and connect. Whether you’re looking to learn new skills, network with professionals, or experience cutting-edge technology or volunteer your time to the community, Digi2U events provide the perfect platform for growth and collaboration.
