The OSHA 30 Construction Certification Training is a 30-hour safety training course designed to equip workers and supervisors with the skills needed to recognize, avoid, and control workplace hazards. Level 1 Basic Training

Course Overview:

The OSHA 30 Construction Certification Training is a comprehensive 30-hour course designed to educate workers and supervisors on workplace safety, hazard prevention, and OSHA compliance. Participants will gain a deep understanding of hazard identification, avoidance, control, and prevention to maintain a safe work environment. This course is ideal for individuals working in construction, project management, and workplace safety enforcement.

  • Course Level: Beginner
  • Skills: Gain essential workplace safety knowledge

Class Projects

Project: Workplace Safety Assessment & Hazard Report Students will conduct a workplace safety assessment, identifying potential hazards in a simulated or real construction environment. They will document their findings in a hazard report, outlining preventive measures, risk controls, and OSHA compliance recommendations to enhance job-site safety.
