01 The Opportunity

The jobs are there.
The on-ramp isn't.

Nearly every job in the American economy now asks for digital skills — and a third of the workforce has never been given the chance to build them. That gap is not a talent problem. It's an access problem, and it falls hardest on the communities we serve.

These are national workforce figures from the Bureau of Labor Statistics and the National Skills Coalition. They describe the landscape Digi2U operates in — they are not Digi2U's results.

02 How Digi2U Responds

We put the equipment
in the neighborhood
and open the door.

No degree, no portfolio, no entrance test. Digi2U runs twelve hands-on learning pathways and three industry certifications out of studios in New Jersey and Atlanta, plus mobile media labs that reach communities without a fixed site. You train on the same printers, cameras, and consoles the industry uses — because that's what makes a skill transferable.
