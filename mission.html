About Digi2U · 501(c)(3) Nonprofit

Access is
the mission.

We're measured not by who we turn away, but by who we bring in and how far they go. Digi2U runs hands-on training in technology, media, and the creative industries — and it's built for working adults from the ground up, not adapted for them after the fact.

Mission & Vision

You already have momentum.

Everyone who walks in here arrives with something — a trade, a hustle, years of work, a good eye. We build on that. Digi2U is a 501(c)(3) preparing people for well-paid technology jobs and in-demand businesses through training in STEAM: Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Math.

M

Our Mission

To equip our communities with the tools, training, and opportunities to succeed.

V

Our Vision

A world where all individuals, regardless of background, have access to the resources needed to thrive in a technology-driven society.

Theory Of Change

Five stages. One pipeline.

This is the spine every Digi2U program hangs from. Learners enter at access and exit as practitioners — and the ones who come back as mentors are what keep the pipeline full.

How The Pipeline Is Funded & Staffed

Four pillars.

Mentorship, apprenticeship, volunteerism, and sponsorship are not add-ons. They are the operating model — the reason programs can run at scale without pricing learners out.

Direction

Where this
is going.

Digi2U operates across four cities today, with mobile media labs extending reach beyond fixed sites and the Studio Way flagship campus anchoring production training in New Jersey and Atlanta.

The next phase is depth over sprawl: deepening certification throughput in existing cities, expanding the apprenticeship pipeline so more graduates return as paid instructors, and growing the mobile lab footprint into communities without a fixed Digi2U site.
